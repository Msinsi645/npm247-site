What we set up, clause by clause

ISO 9001 audits follow clauses 4 to 10 of the standard. We build evidence for every one, so there is nothing the auditor can ask for that you can't show.

  1. 4

    Context of the organisation

    Scope statement, interested parties, internal and external issues, and a process map of how your business actually runs.

  2. 5

    Leadership

    Quality policy, roles and responsibilities, and what top management must show the auditor — including the quality culture expectations added in 2026.

  3. 6

    Planning

    Risk and opportunity register, measurable quality objectives, and a method for planning changes without breaking things.

  4. 7

    Support

    Competence and training records, calibration, document control, and a filing structure your team will keep using.

  5. 8

    Operation

    Procedures for sales, design, purchasing, production or service delivery, supplier evaluation and control of nonconforming output.

  6. 9

    Performance evaluation

    Customer feedback, KPIs, an internal audit programme and a management review that produces decisions, not just minutes.

  7. 10

    Improvement

    Corrective action and root cause analysis, so findings get closed and stay closed.

How it works

A typical small or medium business is audit-ready in three to six months. The first step takes one site visit.

  1. Gap assessment

    We spend a day on site, walk through your operations and measure them against the standard. You get a written report and a fixed quote.

    1–2 weeks

  2. Documentation

    We draft the quality manual, policy, procedures and forms around how you work — then review them with the people who'll use them.

    4–8 weeks

  3. Implementation

    Your team starts working to the new system. We train them, check the records they produce and adjust anything that doesn't fit.

    6–12 weeks

  4. Internal audit and management review

    We run a full internal audit to certification standard and chair your first management review. Findings are closed before the real audit.

    2–3 weeks

  5. Certification audit

    You choose an accredited certification body. We prepare you for stage 1 and stage 2, attend on the day and help you answer any findings.

    Audit dates set by the certification body

Questions we get asked

Do you issue the ISO 9001 certificate?

No. Certificates are issued by independent certification bodies, ideally ones accredited by SANAS or another IAF member. A consultant who also certifies you isn't independent, and the certificate won't carry weight. We prepare you; they audit you.

How long does it take?

Three to six months is typical for a business of up to about 100 people. It depends mostly on how much is already written down and how much time your team can give the project each week.

How much does it cost?

We quote a fixed price after the gap assessment, based on your size, number of sites and how much already exists. The certification body charges separately for its audits.

We're certified to ISO 9001:2015. Do we need to do anything?

Yes, eventually. ISO 9001:2026 was published on 16 September 2026, and a three-year transition period is expected. The changes are evolutionary, but leadership, quality culture and risk all need fresh evidence. A short transition gap assessment tells you exactly what to update.

Will we end up with a pile of documents nobody reads?

Not if we do it properly. We write the minimum the standard requires plus what genuinely helps you run the business, and we test every procedure with the people who'll follow it.

Do you work outside Gauteng?

Yes. We work across South Africa, combining site visits with remote document reviews and online training.
